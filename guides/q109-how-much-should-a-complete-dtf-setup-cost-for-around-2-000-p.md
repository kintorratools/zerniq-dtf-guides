## 4. What Drives the Total Cost at 2,000 Pieces a Month?

At 2,000 pieces a month, your monthly spend is driven by these factors, in this order of impact:

1. **Transfer size.** A 330 mm / 13 in width lets you gang multiple small transfers per pass. A large single transfer consumes film, ink, and powder far faster per piece.
2. **White ink coverage.** CMYK + White means every dark-garment job lays down a white underbase. More white = more ink.
3. **Film consumption per piece.** Measured in linear meters of PET DTF transfer film, and driven entirely by your nesting efficiency.
4. **Powder consumption.** Depends on transfer area, not piece count.
5. **Waste rate.** Failed prints, misfeeds, and test prints. This is where new operators lose the most money.
6. **Equipment amortization.** Printer plus oven, divided over the months you expect to run them.not published
7. **Supporting gear.** Heat press, ventilation, RIP, and workspace fit-out.
8. **Labor and time.** Powdering and curing are hands-on steps.

**Monthly cost model you can fill in yourself:**

- Monthly consumables = (pieces × average transfer area) × (ink + film + powder rate per unit area)
- Monthly equipment = (printer + oven + supporting gear) ÷ amortization months
- Monthly overhead = electricity + maintenance + waste allowance
- **Total monthly cost = consumables + equipment + overhead**

Every rate above must come from a verified ZERNIQ quote or your own measured production data. Do not substitute a guessed figure.
