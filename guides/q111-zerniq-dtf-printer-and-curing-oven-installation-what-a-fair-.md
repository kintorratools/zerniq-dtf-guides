## 8. What a Fair Quote Should Itemize

A fair DTF installation quote should separate every major cost center:

- **Site survey:** Who visits, what is measured, and what is documented.
- **Electrical scope:** Circuits, outlets, breaker, conduit, grounding, permit, inspection.
- **Ventilation scope:** Exhaust, ducting, fan, make-up air, filtration, fire safety.
- **Equipment placement:** Printer, oven, bench, storage, workflow layout.
- **Setup:** Unpacking, leveling, ink installation, powder unit, film loading.
- **Calibration:** Nozzle check, test print, curing test, transfer test.
- **RIP and color:** Software setup and profile support.
- **Training:** Operator training and safety training.
- **Consumables:** Ink, film, powder, and cleaning supplies included at startup.
- **Warranty:** What is covered, for how long, and who pays labor.
- **Exclusions:** What is not included, such as panel upgrades, duct modifications, or fire suppression.
- **Payment milestones:** Deposit, delivery, installation completion, acceptance.

A quote with clear exclusions is safer than a vague all-in quote. A vague quote often becomes expensive later.
