## 2. Quick Definition: What Counts as a Complete DTF Workflow?

**A complete DTF workflow is a four-part production line: a DTF printer, PET DTF transfer film, DTF powder, and a curing oven, followed by a heat press.**

Key parts:

- **DTF printer:** prints the mirror-image design onto film.
- **PET DTF transfer film:** receives the print; roll film allows continuous production.
- **DTF powder:** hot-melt adhesive powder applied to the wet print.
- **Curing oven:** melts and cures the powder so the transfer can bond to fabric.
- **Heat press:** transfers the cured design onto bags, workwear, or other garments.

For an easy-run setup, the printer, film, powder, and oven must be treated as one system—not four unrelated purchases.
