## 11. Build Your $5,000 DTF Setup: Numbered Steps

1. Set your maximum print size. Write down the largest T-shirt design you plan to sell. If it exceeds **330 mm / 13 in** wide, this A3 class needs tiling or a different machine.
2. Choose the printer. For verified starter specs, the ZERNIQ XP600 A3 DTF lists **XP600** printhead, **1440 × 1440 dpi**, **CMYK + White DTF ink**, and **PET DTF transfer film roll continuous printing**.
3. Add a heat press. Confirm the platen size covers your transfer and that temperature/pressure controls are repeatable. not published
4. Add powder handling. Decide manual powder application or an automated shaker/oven. Manual saves equipment cost but adds labor and inconsistency risk.
5. Budget PET DTF film. Use roll film if you want continuous printing. Confirm roll width, core size, and printer compatibility.
6. Budget CMYK + White DTF ink. White ink is essential for dark garments but needs circulation and maintenance habits.
7. Budget adhesive powder and curing. Confirm powder type and curing method match your film and ink.
8. Keep a reserve. Set aside part of the **$5,000** for shipping, taxes, initial mistakes, spare consumables, and maintenance. not published
9. Get written quotes. Compare printer, press, powder, film, ink, training, warranty, and support in one spreadsheet.
10. Test before scaling. Print light shirts, dark shirts, fine detail, and wash tests before taking large orders.
